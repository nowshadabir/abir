Our Green Story

Founded in 2024 by two friends who realized the transformative power of nature in urban spaces. Our mission is simple: to make every home a blooming oasis.

Mission & Sourcing

We believe that beautiful plants shouldn't come at the cost of the environment. That's why we've partnered with local, sustainable nurseries that prioritize responsible cultivation.

Eco-Friendly Packaging: Every order is shipped using 100% recyclable materials, including biodegradable padding and compostable soil bags.
