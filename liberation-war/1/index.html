Bangladesh Liberation War Archive

Welcome to the online archive of the 1971 Liberation War. We preserve history.

Introduction

The Liberation War of Bangladesh was a revolution and armed conflict sparked by the rise of the Bengali nationalist and self-determination movement in what was then East Pakistan during the 1971 Bangladesh genocide.

This archive is created to show the importance of our history to the new generation.
