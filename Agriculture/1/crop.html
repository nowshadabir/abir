Detailed Crop Information

Fruits

Growth Cycles: Fruit crops progress through establishment, vegetative, and reproductive phases. Success depends on:

  • Seasonal pruning schedules
  • Balanced fertilization
  • Consistent moisture control

Vegetables

Cultivation: Vegetables thrive in organic-rich environments. Key factors include:

  • Soil pH (6.0 - 7.0)
  • Direct vs indirect sunlight
  • Optimal drainage systems
