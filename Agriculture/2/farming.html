Seasonal Farming Guides

Information on managing your farm activities through different seasons:

Spring

Focus on soil preparation, seed sowing, and early irrigation to kickstart the growth cycle.

Summer

Emphasis on weed control, pest monitoring, and intensive watering to handle the peak heat.

Autumn

Main activities include harvesting mature crops and enriching the soil for the next season.

Winter

Maintenance of equipment, greenhouse management, and allowing the land to rest and recover.
