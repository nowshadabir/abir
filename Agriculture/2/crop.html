Crop Details

Detailed information on various crop types for better management.

Fruits

Fruit crops go through distinct stages from establishment to the reproductive phase. Proper maintenance is essential for high-quality production.

  • Growth Cycle: Includes planting, blooming, and fruit ripening stages.
  • Maintenance: Regular pruning, consistent watering, and pest monitoring.

Vegetables

Vegetables have specific soil and sunlight needs. Understanding these requirements helps in achieving better yields and healthier plants.

  • Soil Requirements: Needs nutrient-rich, well-draining soil with a pH of 6.0 to 7.0.
  • Cultivation: Regular weeding and appropriate spacing between plants.

Cash Crops

These crops are grown primarily for commercial value and often require large-scale harvesting techniques.

  • Economic Value: Vital for national and international trade markets.
  • Harvesting Methods: Use of specialized machinery to ensure efficiency.
