Pest Control

Strategies for managing common agricultural pests effectively.

Monitoring

Regular field scouting and the use of pheromone traps allow for early detection of pest threats before they can cause significant crop damage.

Treatment

Focus on biological control methods, such as introducing natural predators like ladybugs or using organic Neem oil sprays to manage populations.

IPM Techniques

Integrated Pest Management (IPM) uses a combination of biological, cultural, and mechanical tools to minimize economic and environmental risks.
