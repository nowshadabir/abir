Pest Management Strategies

Early Detection & Monitoring

Proactive field scouting is essential for identifying pest threats before they escalate into significant outbreaks. Regular monitoring allows for timely and targeted interventions.

Biological Control Methods

Using natural enemies and organic treatments to manage pest populations while maintaining ecological balance and minimizing the use of synthetic chemicals.

Integrated Pest Management (IPM)

A holistic and sustainable approach that combines biological, cultural, and mechanical tools for long-term pest prevention. Key components include:

  • Prevention
  • Threshold Monitoring
  • Control Strategies
